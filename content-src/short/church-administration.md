# Administration as ministry | Order that serves people.
## Decently and in order | 1 Corinthians 14:40; 1 Corinthians 12:28; Exodus 18:13-26
"Let all things be done decently and in order" (1 Corinthians 14:40). Among God's gifts to the church Paul lists "helps, governments" (1 Corinthians 12:28): the Greek word behind "governments" pictures a helmsman steering a ship.

Administration is not unspiritual paperwork. When Moses was overwhelmed, Jethro's advice was administrative: appoint capable leaders over thousands, hundreds, fifties and tens, with a clear system for bringing hard cases up (Exodus 18:21-22). The result: "this people shall also go to their place in peace" (verse 23).

Good administration:

- **Serves people** and frees leaders for prayer and the Word (Acts 6:2-4).
- **Builds trust** through transparency.
- **Prevents chaos**, waste and conflict.
- **Multiplies ministry** by organising many hands.
## Structures and roles | Acts 6:1-7; Numbers 1:1-3; Romans 12:6-8
In Acts 6, a complaint about neglected widows was solved by a structure: seven men "of honest report" appointed "over this business". The result: "the word of God increased" (Acts 6:7).

Building structure:

- **Organisational chart**: who leads what, and who reports to whom.
- **Role descriptions** for each office and department: purpose, duties, authority, reporting line.
- **Governing documents**: constitution or bylaws, statement of faith, policies.
- **Decision-making**: what the pastor decides, what the board/council decides, what the congregation decides.
- **Departments**: worship, children, youth, evangelism, welfare, media, finance, ushering, facilities.

Keep structures **simple** and suited to your size. A house church of 30 needs fewer offices than a congregation of 3,000. Structures should serve the mission, not become the mission.

# Meetings, records and people | Running the day-to-day well.
## Meetings and records | Ezra 6:1-3; Luke 1:3; Proverbs 27:23
When the rebuilding of the temple was challenged, Darius "made a decree, and search was made in the house of the rolls", and Cyrus' decree was found (Ezra 6:1-3). Records protected God's work.

**Effective meetings**:

- Clear **purpose** and **agenda** sent in advance.
- Start and end **on time**; open with prayer and Scripture.
- A **chairperson** who keeps focus and gives everyone a voice.
- Decisions recorded with **action points**: who does what by when.
- **Minutes** circulated within a few days and confirmed at the next meeting.

**Records to keep**:

- Membership (with consent), attendance, baptisms, marriages, dedications, deaths.
- Minutes, policies and correspondence.
- Financial records (see the finances course).
- Assets inventory and property documents.

**Data protection**: keep personal data secure, collect only what you need, and respect members' privacy, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
## Volunteers | Nehemiah 3:1-32; Romans 12:4-8; 1 Peter 4:10
Nehemiah 3 lists families, priests, goldsmiths, perfume-makers, rulers and daughters, each repairing a section of the wall "over against his house". Many hands, clear assignments.

Managing volunteers:

- **Recruit** personally and by gifting, not only by announcements.
- **Place** people where their gifts fit (Romans 12:6).
- **Train**: orientation, clear expectations, skills.
- **Schedule** fairly with rotas so no one burns out.
- **Communicate** regularly.
- **Appreciate** publicly and privately.
- **Care**: volunteers are people to be shepherded, not tools.
- **Screen** those working with children and vulnerable people.

"As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards" (1 Peter 4:10).

# Facilities, events, communication and compliance | Practical and legal basics.
## Facilities, events and communication | Luke 14:28-30; 1 Chronicles 28:11-13; Habakkuk 2:2
David gave Solomon "the pattern" of the temple, with detailed plans for its rooms, treasuries and courses of service (1 Chronicles 28:11-13). Planning honours God.

**Facilities**: maintenance schedules, safety (fire extinguishers, exits, electrical checks, generator safety), cleanliness, accessibility for the elderly and disabled, and security.

**Events checklist**: purpose, date, venue, budget, programme, speakers, permissions, publicity, logistics (chairs, sound, power, water), safety and first aid, volunteers, follow-up, and a review afterwards.

**Communication**:

- Announcements that are short and clear.
- A weekly bulletin or WhatsApp broadcast.
- A church calendar shared in advance.
- Website and social media kept up to date.
- A designated contact for enquiries.

"Write the vision, and make it plain" (Habakkuk 2:2). Members cannot support what they do not know about.
## Legal and regulatory basics | Romans 13:1-7; Matthew 22:21; Titus 3:1
"Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's" (Matthew 22:21). "Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates" (Titus 3:1).

In Nigeria, churches and ministries commonly register with the **Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)** as an **Incorporated Trustees** body under Part F of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020. This gives legal status to own property and open accounts. Requirements change, so seek current advice from a lawyer or accredited agent.

Compliance basics:

- Keep governing documents and trustee records up to date; file **annual returns** with the CAC.
- Proper **bank accounts** in the organisation's name.
- **Employment** obligations for staff (contracts, PAYE tax, pension where applicable).
- **Property** documents and building approvals.
- **Event permits** where required.
- **Data protection** and **safeguarding** policies.

Integrity in compliance protects the ministry's witness.
