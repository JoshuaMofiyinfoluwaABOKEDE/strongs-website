# The call to holiness | Holy, as He is holy.
## Be ye holy | 1 Peter 1:15-16; Leviticus 11:44; Hebrews 12:14
"As he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation; Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy" (1 Peter 1:15-16, quoting Leviticus 11:44). "Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord" (Hebrews 12:14).

Holiness means **set apart for God** and **morally pure**, sharing God's character. It flows from who God is: holy, holy, holy (Isaiah 6:3).

Some teachers emphasise holiness more than others, but all faithful Christian traditions agree that believers are called to turn from sin and become like Christ. William Kumuyi's ministry has long made holiness a central theme, urging believers not to accept sin as normal but to expect real victory through Christ.

Holiness is not:

- Mere outward rules about dress or food.
- Sinless perfection that never needs confession (1 John 1:8-9).
- A way to earn salvation.

It is a **heart** turned fully to God and a **life** that increasingly matches His character.
## Saved to be holy | Romans 6:1-14; Titus 2:11-14; 2 Corinthians 5:17
"Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? God forbid. How shall we, that are dead to sin, live any longer therein?" (Romans 6:1-2).

Grace does not excuse sin; it **teaches** us to deny it: "the grace of God that bringeth salvation... Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world" (Titus 2:11-12). Christ "gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works" (2:14).

Two aspects:

- **Positional holiness**: in Christ, believers are already set apart and declared holy (1 Corinthians 6:11, "ye are sanctified").
- **Progressive holiness**: day by day the Spirit makes us more like Christ (2 Corinthians 3:18).

"If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature" (2 Corinthians 5:17). New life produces new living.

# How holiness grows | The cross, the Spirit and the Word.
## The way of the cross | Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:6-11; Luke 9:23
"I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me" (Galatians 2:20). "Our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin" (Romans 6:6).

Gbile Akanni's teaching has repeatedly pointed to the cross as the path of true holiness: not trying harder in our own strength, but dying to self and letting Christ live through us.

In practice:

- **Reckon** yourself dead to sin and alive to God (Romans 6:11): believe what God says about you.
- **Deny self** daily (Luke 9:23): say no to selfish desires.
- **Yield** your body to God as an instrument of righteousness (Romans 6:13).
- **Accept** the discipline of God, which is "for our profit, that we might be partakers of his holiness" (Hebrews 12:10).
## The Spirit and the Word | Galatians 5:16-25; Psalm 119:9-11; John 17:17
"Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh" (Galatians 5:16). Holiness is the Spirit's fruit, not our achievement (5:22-23).

"Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth" (John 17:17). "Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word... Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee" (Psalm 119:9, 11).

Means of growth:

- **Daily Bible reading and memorisation**.
- **Prayer** and confession.
- **Fellowship** and accountability with other believers.
- **Obedience** in small things.
- **Worship** and the Lord's Supper.
- **Avoiding** what feeds the flesh: certain media, company and places.
- **Filling the mind** with what is pure (Philippians 4:8).

# Victory and witness | Overcoming temptation and shining as lights.
## Overcoming temptation | 1 Corinthians 10:13; James 1:13-15; Matthew 4:1-11
"There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape" (1 Corinthians 10:13).

Temptation grows in stages: desire, enticement, conception, sin, death (James 1:14-15). Stop it early.

Jesus overcame temptation with Scripture: "It is written" (Matthew 4:4, 7, 10).

Key areas and safeguards:

- **Sexual purity**: flee (2 Timothy 2:22); guard eyes and devices; avoid compromising situations.
- **Honesty**: no exam malpractice, bribery, padding of expenses or lies.
- **Money**: contentment; avoid greed and get-rich-quick schemes.
- **Tongue**: no gossip, cursing or slander (James 3).
- **Anger and unforgiveness**: deal with them quickly (Ephesians 4:26, 32).
- **Pride**: humble yourself before God (James 4:6-10).

When you fall, **confess quickly** (1 John 1:9), make restitution where needed, and get up.
## Holy lives that commend the gospel | Matthew 5:14-16; 1 Peter 2:11-12; Philippians 2:14-16
"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven" (Matthew 5:16). Peter urges believers to have "your conversation honest among the Gentiles: that... they may by your good works, which they shall behold, glorify God" (1 Peter 2:12).

Holiness and evangelism belong together:

- A holy life makes the message **believable**.
- Hypocrisy **closes** ears to the gospel.
- Integrity at work and school opens **doors** for witness.
- Changed lives are **evidence** of the gospel's power.

Paul called believers to be "blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world; Holding forth the word of life" (Philippians 2:15-16).

The world watches how Christians do business, treat staff, handle money, speak online and treat the poor. Holy living is itself preaching.
