# The commission | Disciples, not just decisions.
## Go and make disciples | Matthew 28:18-20; Acts 14:21-22; Colossians 1:28-29
"All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them... Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you" (Matthew 28:18-20). The main verb is **make disciples**; going, baptising and teaching describe how.

A **disciple** is a learner and follower of Jesus who:

- Has put their trust in Him (conversion).
- Is **obeying** His commands (teaching "to observe", not just to know).
- Is **growing** in Christlikeness.
- Is **reproducing**: helping others follow Jesus.

Paul and Barnabas "had preached the gospel to that city, and had taught many", then returned "confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith" (Acts 14:21-22). Paul laboured "that we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus" (Colossians 1:28).

Gbile Akanni's ministry has repeatedly warned that churches full of converts but empty of disciples are weak, and has called believers to the costly, patient work of raising disciples.
## The cost of following | Luke 14:25-33; Mark 8:34-38; John 8:31-32
"Whosoever doth not bear his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple... whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple" (Luke 14:27, 33).

Jesus never hid the cost. Discipleship means:

- **Christ first**, above family, possessions and self (Luke 14:26).
- **Taking up the cross**: daily death to self (Mark 8:34).
- **Continuing in His word**: "If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed" (John 8:31).
- **Love for one another** (John 13:35).
- **Bearing fruit** (John 15:8).

We must teach this cost honestly from the beginning. A gospel without the cross produces shallow followers who fall away under pressure (Mark 4:16-17). A disciple who has counted the cost stands.

# Jesus' way | How the Master made disciples.
## Chosen to be with Him | Mark 3:13-15; John 1:35-51; Luke 6:12-13
Jesus prayed all night, then chose twelve (Luke 6:12-13), "that they should be with him, and that he might send them forth to preach" (Mark 3:14).

Elements of Jesus' method:

- **Prayerful selection** of a few.
- **Invitation**: "Come and see" (John 1:39); "Follow me" (Mark 1:17).
- **Association**: they lived, walked, ate and travelled with Him.
- **Demonstration**: they watched Him pray, teach, heal, rebuke, forgive, serve.
- **Instruction**: public teaching and private explanation (Mark 4:34).
- **Delegation**: He sent them out (Luke 9:1-6; 10:1-20).
- **Supervision**: they reported back; He corrected and encouraged.
- **Reproduction**: He commissioned them to do the same (Matthew 28:19).

Jesus spent most of His time with a few, so that through them He could reach the many.
## Life-on-life discipleship | 1 Thessalonians 2:7-12; Philippians 4:9; 1 Corinthians 11:1
"Be ye followers of me, even as I also am of Christ" (1 Corinthians 11:1). Paul shared "not the gospel of God only, but also our own souls" (1 Thessalonians 2:8). "Those things, which ye have both learned, and received, and heard, and seen in me, do" (Philippians 4:9).

Discipleship is caught as much as taught. It happens through **shared life**:

- Meeting regularly to read Scripture and pray together.
- Taking disciples along: visits, outreach, errands, family time.
- Letting them see how you handle money, conflict, failure and pressure.
- Asking honest questions and allowing honest answers.
- Correcting gently and celebrating growth.

Gbile Akanni's ministry has emphasised that disciples are formed through close, honest relationships and the example of lives laid down, not through programmes alone.

# Multiplying disciples | To the third and fourth generation.
## A practical discipleship plan | 2 Timothy 3:10-17; Hebrews 5:12-14; Ephesians 4:11-16
Paul reminded Timothy, "thou hast fully known my doctrine, manner of life, purpose, faith, longsuffering, charity, patience, persecutions, afflictions" (2 Timothy 3:10-11). Discipleship touches all of life.

A six-month discipleship plan for one person or a group of three to five:

- **Weekly meeting** (60 to 90 minutes): Scripture, discussion, prayer, accountability questions.
- **Foundations** (months 1 to 2): assurance, prayer, the Word, the Holy Spirit, the church, holiness.
- **Growth** (months 3 to 4): a book of the Bible (e.g. Mark or Philippians); spiritual disciplines; dealing with sin; relationships.
- **Ministry** (months 5 to 6): sharing the gospel, serving, beginning to disciple someone else.
- **Shared life**: outreach together, service projects, meals.
- **Accountability questions**: How is your walk with God? What is God teaching you? Where are you tempted? Who are you sharing with?

Move from "milk" to "meat" (Hebrews 5:12-14).
## Four generations | 2 Timothy 2:1-2; Acts 19:9-10; 1 Thessalonians 1:6-8
"The things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also" (2 Timothy 2:2). Four generations: **Paul → Timothy → faithful men → others**.

Multiplication is slow at first but powerful: if each disciple helps one other become a reproducing disciple each year, the numbers grow faster than addition ever could.

Building a multiplying culture:

- **Expect reproduction** from the start: every disciple will make disciples.
- **Use simple, reproducible tools** that a new disciple can use with others.
- **Release** disciples to lead early, with support.
- **Track generations**: who is discipling whom?
- **Celebrate** third and fourth generation disciples.
- **Stay humble**: God gives the growth (1 Corinthians 3:6).

The Thessalonians, newly discipled, became "ensamples to all that believe", and "from you sounded out the word of the Lord" (1 Thessalonians 1:7-8).
