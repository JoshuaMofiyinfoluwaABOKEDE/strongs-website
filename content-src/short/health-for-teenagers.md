# Your body, His temple | Caring for the body God gave you.
## Fearfully and wonderfully made | Psalm 139:13-16; 1 Corinthians 6:19-20
"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works" (Psalm 139:14). God formed you in your mother's womb and saw you before you were born. Your body is not an accident or a burden; it is His design.

"Know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you... ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body" (1 Corinthians 6:19-20).

So caring for your body is not vanity. It is stewardship and worship. That includes:

- **Eating well** and drinking enough water.
- **Moving**: sports, walking, dancing, play.
- **Sleeping** enough: teenagers need about 8 to 10 hours.
- **Keeping clean**.
- **Avoiding harm**: drugs, smoking, alcohol, and risky behaviour.

Social media often shows edited, unrealistic bodies. Don't compare yourself with filters. God made you with purpose.
## Food, sleep and hygiene | Daniel 1:12-15; Psalm 127:2
Daniel and his friends asked for simple food and water and after ten days "their countenances appeared fairer and fatter in flesh than all the children which did eat the portion of the king's meat" (Daniel 1:15). What you eat matters.

**Food**:

- Eat **regular meals**, including breakfast.
- Include **vegetables and fruit**, **proteins** (beans, eggs, fish, meat), and **whole grains**.
- Limit sugary drinks, sweets, and too much fried or processed food.
- Drink **water** through the day.

**Sleep**: "he giveth his beloved sleep" (Psalm 127:2). Sleep helps you grow, learn and stay well. Put phones away an hour before bed; keep a regular sleep time.

**Hygiene**:

- Bath daily; use deodorant as you grow.
- Brush teeth twice a day.
- Wash hands with soap.
- Change into clean clothes and underwear daily.
- Girls: change sanitary pads regularly during periods.

# Growing up | Puberty, emotions and relationships.
## Changes of puberty | Luke 2:52; 1 Corinthians 13:11
"Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man" (Luke 2:52). Growing up is part of God's plan, even for Jesus.

**Puberty** is the time (usually between about 9 and 16) when your body changes from a child's to an adult's. Changes are normal, and they happen at different times for different people:

- **Everyone**: growth spurts, body hair, sweating and body odour, spots or acne, mood changes.
- **Girls**: breasts develop, hips widen, and **menstruation** (periods) begins. Periods are normal and healthy. Track them; talk to a trusted adult or health worker if they are very painful, very heavy, or very irregular.
- **Boys**: voice deepens, shoulders broaden, facial hair grows, and the body produces sperm. Night-time emissions ("wet dreams") are normal.

If you have questions, ask a parent, a trusted adult, a school nurse or a doctor rather than relying on rumours or the internet. There is no shame in asking.
## Relationships and purity | 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Timothy 2:22; Proverbs 4:23
"Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity" (1 Timothy 4:12).

As you grow, attraction to others is normal. God's design for sex is within marriage, and purity protects your heart, health and future. "Flee also youthful lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace" (2 Timothy 2:22).

Wise boundaries:

- **Choose friends** who help you follow God.
- **Avoid situations** that make temptation easy: being alone in private rooms, late nights, parties with alcohol.
- **Guard your eyes online**: avoid pornography; use filters and keep devices in shared spaces.
- **Never share intimate photos**, and don't forward any you receive.
- **Your body is yours to protect**. No one has the right to touch you inappropriately or pressure you, including online. Say no, leave, and **tell a trusted adult**. It's never your fault.

If you have fallen, God forgives and restores (1 John 1:9). Talk to a trusted adult or pastor.

# Mind and choices | Mental health, substances and online life.
## Your mind matters | Philippians 4:6-8; Psalm 34:18; Proverbs 12:25
"Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad" (Proverbs 12:25). Feelings of stress, sadness, worry and anger are part of being human. Even David, Elijah and Jeremiah went through deep sadness.

Healthy ways to care for your mind:

- **Pray and bring worries to God** (Philippians 4:6-7).
- **Talk** to a parent, friend, teacher, pastor or counsellor. Speaking helps.
- **Think on good things** (Philippians 4:8): limit news and social media that make you anxious.
- **Move your body** and spend time outside.
- **Sleep** and eat well.
- **Make time for rest and fun.**

**Get help** if sadness or worry doesn't go away for weeks, if you stop enjoying things, can't sleep or eat, or feel hopeless. Talk to a trusted adult, and they can help you see a doctor or counsellor. If you ever feel unsafe or think about hurting yourself, tell an adult **immediately** or call **112**. "The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart" (Psalm 34:18).
## Drugs, alcohol and online life | Proverbs 20:1; Proverbs 23:29-32; Ephesians 5:15-18
"Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise" (Proverbs 20:1).

**Substances**: drugs (including tramadol, codeine syrup, cannabis, and others), smoking, vaping and alcohol can harm the developing brain, lead to addiction, and cause accidents, crime and lost opportunities.

- **Know your "no"** in advance: "No thanks, I don't do that."
- **Leave** places where drugs are being used.
- Only take medicine prescribed for **you**, as directed.
- If you or a friend is struggling with substances, **tell a trusted adult**. Help is available and recovery is possible.

**Online life**:

- **Limit screen time**; set phone-free times.
- **Protect privacy**: don't share your address, school, location or passwords.
- **Beware of strangers** online; people can pretend to be someone else. Never meet an online contact alone.
- **Be kind**: don't bully or forward hurtful content.
- **Think before you post**: it may last forever.

"See then that ye walk circumspectly, not as fools, but as wise, redeeming the time" (Ephesians 5:15-16).

@assignment Practical assignment: my healthy habits plan
Write a 300 to 500 word plan for the next month covering:

1. **Body**: your food, water, exercise and sleep goals.
2. **Growing up**: one question you will ask a trusted adult, and who that person is.
3. **Relationships and purity**: two boundaries you will keep, with a Bible verse.
4. **Mind**: how you will handle stress, and who you will talk to when you struggle.
5. **Choices**: your plan for screen time and saying no to substances.

You do not need to share private details. Keep a simple weekly checklist and add a 150-word reflection at the end of the month.
