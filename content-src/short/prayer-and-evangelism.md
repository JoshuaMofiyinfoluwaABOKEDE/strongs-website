# Prayer before people | Why evangelism begins on our knees.
## Prayer opens the way | Acts 1:14; Acts 2:41; Acts 4:29-31
The church in Acts prayed before it preached. The disciples "continued with one accord in prayer and supplication" (Acts 1:14); at Pentecost three thousand were saved (Acts 2:41). When threatened, they prayed, "grant unto thy servants, that with all boldness they may speak thy word", and "they spake the word of God with boldness" (Acts 4:29, 31).

Why prayer is essential to evangelism:

- **Salvation is God's work**: "No man can come to me, except the Father which hath sent me draw him" (John 6:44).
- **Spiritual blindness** must be lifted: "the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not" (2 Corinthians 4:4).
- **The Holy Spirit convicts** (John 16:8).
- **Workers need boldness and wisdom**.
- **Doors must open** (Colossians 4:3).

Reinhard Bonnke's crusades were supported by large prayer teams, and he often said in various ways that evangelism without prayer achieves little. "Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it" (Psalm 127:1).
## Jesus, the praying evangelist | Mark 1:35-39; Luke 6:12-13; Luke 22:31-32
After a busy evening of healing, Jesus rose "a great while before day" to pray. When the disciples found Him, He said, "Let us go into the next towns, that I may preach there also" (Mark 1:35-38). Prayer led Him to the next towns.

He prayed all night before choosing the twelve (Luke 6:12-13). He prayed for Peter by name: "I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not" (Luke 22:32). On the cross He prayed for those crucifying Him: "Father, forgive them" (Luke 23:34).

Lessons:

- Prayer gives **direction** for mission.
- Prayer is **specific** and personal.
- Prayer is **compassionate**, even for enemies.

If Jesus, the perfect evangelist, depended on prayer, how much more must we.

# What to pray | Specific prayers for the lost and the work.
## Praying for the lost by name | Romans 10:1; 1 Timothy 2:1-4; 2 Corinthians 4:3-6
Paul wrote, "Brethren, my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved" (Romans 10:1). God "will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth" (1 Timothy 2:4).

Keep a **prayer list** of unsaved people (family, friends, colleagues, neighbours). Pray for them **daily** and **specifically**:

- That God would **draw** them (John 6:44).
- That **blindness** would be lifted and they would see "the light of the glorious gospel of Christ" (2 Corinthians 4:4-6).
- That the Spirit would **convict** them (John 16:8).
- That they would **hear** the gospel clearly, from you or others.
- That **hindrances** (fear, pride, family pressure, wrong teaching) would be removed.
- That they would **repent and believe**.
- That you would have **opportunities** and words.

Many people have been saved after years of a mother's, friend's or pastor's prayers. Keep praying.
## Praying for labourers, doors and boldness | Matthew 9:37-38; Colossians 4:2-4; Ephesians 6:18-20
Jesus told us to "pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest" (Matthew 9:38).

Paul asked for prayer for his evangelism:

- "that God would open unto us a door of utterance, to speak the mystery of Christ... That I may make it manifest, as I ought to speak" (Colossians 4:3-4): **open doors** and **clarity**.
- "that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly" (Ephesians 6:19): **boldness**.
- "that the word of the Lord may have free course, and be glorified" (2 Thessalonians 3:1): **the spread of the Word**.
- "that we may be delivered from unreasonable and wicked men" (2 Thessalonians 3:2): **protection**.

Pray these for yourself, your team, evangelists and missionaries you know, and STRONGS outreaches. Then expect God to answer, and be ready to be one of the labourers.

# Prayer in action | Prayer walking, warfare and outreach strategy.
## Prayer walking and spiritual warfare | Joshua 1:3; Ephesians 6:10-18; 2 Corinthians 10:3-5
"Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you" (Joshua 1:3). **Prayer walking** means praying on-site as you walk through a street, campus, market or village.

How to prayer walk:

- Go in twos or threes; walk normally and pray quietly or conversationally.
- Pray for what you see: homes, schools, businesses, hospitals, people.
- Bless people and places in Jesus' name.
- Be sensitive to opportunities to greet, help or share.
- Record impressions and needs for later prayer and outreach.

**Spiritual warfare**: "we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers" (Ephesians 6:12). Our weapons are spiritual: truth, righteousness, the gospel, faith, salvation, the Word and prayer (Ephesians 6:14-18). We pull down strongholds of wrong thinking with the truth (2 Corinthians 10:4-5). Focus on Christ's victory, not on fear or speculation about demons.
## A prayer strategy for outreach | Acts 13:1-4; Acts 12:5; Acts 16:25-34
The Antioch church prayed and fasted before sending missionaries (Acts 13:2-3). The church prayed "without ceasing" for Peter in prison (Acts 12:5). Paul and Silas prayed and sang at midnight, and the jailer and his house were saved (Acts 16:25-34).

Build prayer into every stage of outreach:

- **Before**: weeks of prayer meetings; prayer walking the area; personal prayer lists; fasting where appropriate.
- **During**: a dedicated prayer team praying throughout; team members praying before each conversation; prayer for people on the spot when they welcome it.
- **After**: prayer for new believers by name, for follow-up workers and for receiving churches; thanksgiving for answers.

Recruit **prayer partners** for every outreach team member. Share specific requests and answers to build faith.

"Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving" (Colossians 4:2).
