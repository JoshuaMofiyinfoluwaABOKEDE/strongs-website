# Planning your presence | Purpose, audience and pillars.
## A mission for your content | Matthew 5:14-16; Colossians 4:5-6
"Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid" (Matthew 5:14). The internet is a hill. What we post can reach thousands.

Before you start, write a **content mission** in one sentence: "I write to help Nigerian university students follow Jesus in their studies and relationships."

Then define:

- **Audience**: who exactly? Age, situation, questions, struggles.
- **Platforms**: where are they? A blog for depth; WhatsApp for community; Instagram, TikTok or YouTube Shorts for short visuals; X and Threads for short text; LinkedIn for professionals; Facebook for broad reach.
- **Content pillars**: three to five themes you'll return to (e.g. Bible study tips, campus life, purpose and career, testimonies).
- **Frequency**: what can you sustain? One good post a week beats seven rushed ones.

"Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time" (Colossians 4:5).
## Setting up a blog | Habakkuk 2:2; Proverbs 24:3-4
A blog is your home base: you own it, and posts stay findable for years.

- **Platforms**: WordPress.com, Blogger, Medium, Substack (blog plus email newsletter). Free plans are fine to start.
- **Name**: short, clear, memorable.
- **About page**: who you are, what you write about, and why.
- **Simple design**: readable font, clear menu, works on phones.
- **Categories** matching your content pillars.
- **Contact** or subscribe option.

**Search (SEO) basics**:

- Use the words your readers would search for in your **title** and first paragraph ("How to study the Bible as a busy student").
- Write clear **headings**.
- Add a short **description** and an image with **alt text**.
- Link to your other related posts.

Write first for people, then make it easy for search engines to find.

# Writing blog posts | Headlines, structure and calls to action.
## Headlines and openings | Proverbs 25:25; Ecclesiastes 12:10
Most people decide from the **headline** whether to read.

Headline types that work:

- **How to**: "How to Pray When You Don't Feel Like It"
- **Lists**: "5 Lessons from Daniel for Final-Year Students"
- **Questions**: "Is It Wrong to Be Ambitious?"
- **Specific promises**: "A 10-Minute Bible Study Plan for Busy Mornings"

Be **honest**: never use clickbait that the post doesn't deliver.

**Openings**: hook readers in the first two lines with a story, a question, a surprising fact, or the reader's problem stated clearly. Then tell them what the post will give them.
## Structure, style and action | Nehemiah 8:8; James 1:22
Online readers **scan**. Help them:

- **Short paragraphs** (one to three sentences).
- **Subheadings** every 200 to 300 words.
- **Lists** and **bold** for key points (sparingly).
- **Images** that add meaning.
- **Plain, conversational language**: write as if talking to a friend.
- **Scripture** quoted accurately with references.
- **Length**: 700 to 1,200 words for most posts; longer for in-depth guides.

**End with action** (James 1:22, "be ye doers"):

- A clear takeaway or next step.
- A question for comments.
- A link to a related post.
- An invitation to subscribe or share.

Proofread on your phone before publishing; that's how most will read it.

# Social media and integrity | Platform writing, growth and guarding your heart.
## Writing for each platform | 1 Corinthians 9:22; Proverbs 15:23
"I am made all things to all men, that I might by all means save some" (1 Corinthians 9:22). Each platform has its own language.

- **Instagram / Facebook captions**: a strong first line (it's all people see before "more"), short paragraphs, a question or call to action, a few relevant hashtags.
- **WhatsApp status and channels**: very short; one idea; often an image with a few words.
- **X / Threads**: one sharp sentence, or a thread of short numbered posts that build an argument or story.
- **Short video scripts** (Reels, TikTok, Shorts): hook in the first 2 seconds, one point, 30 to 60 seconds, captions on screen, a clear ending.
- **LinkedIn**: professional tone, a lesson from experience, short lines.

**Repurpose**: one blog post can become a carousel, three quotes, a short video and a thread.

Always add **alt text** or captions for accessibility.
## Growth, integrity and wellbeing | Galatians 1:10; Philippians 4:8; Psalm 46:10
"Do I seek to please men? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ" (Galatians 1:10).

**Growing honestly**:

- Post **consistently**.
- **Engage**: reply to comments and messages kindly.
- **Collaborate** with others who share your mission.
- **Never buy followers** or use fake engagement.
- **Check facts** before sharing; don't spread rumours or fake news.
- **Credit** images, quotes and ideas.

**Integrity online**:

- Be the same person online and offline.
- Disagree respectfully; don't join pile-ons.
- Protect privacy: yours and others'. Get consent before posting people, especially children.
- Avoid boasting and comparison.

**Wellbeing**: likes are not your worth. Set screen-time limits, take regular breaks (a weekly digital Sabbath), and keep real-life relationships. "Be still, and know that I am God" (Psalm 46:10). "Whatsoever things are true... think on these things" (Philippians 4:8).
